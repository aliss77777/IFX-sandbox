# Project Brief: Huge IFX Soccer

### Executive Summary

The Huge IFX Soccer project is a publicly demo-able AI application designed to showcase Huge's Intelligent Fan Experiences (IX) principles. It will feature a fictional soccer league to accelerate the sales pipeline and demonstrate our unique capabilities.

The primary problem we are solving is Huge's current inability to share its most impressive AI work due to NDAs. This demo will provide a tangible example of our expertise, addressing the sports industry's struggle to engage younger fans and effectively leverage data.

While the application itself is designed to appeal to Millennial and Gen Z sports fans, the primary target audience for this demo is potential clients: sports leagues, teams, venues, and sponsors.

The key value proposition is to prove Huge's ability to drive top-line growth for sports organizations. We will demonstrate how our AI-powered, fan-centric experiences can attract and retain the next generation of fans, positioning Huge as a strategic innovation partner, not just a vendor.

### Problem Statement

**The Industry Problem: A Disconnected Fan Experience**

The sports industry faces a critical challenge: its fan base is aging, and the attention of younger audiences is fragmented across countless digital platforms. Sports organizations are sitting on mountains of data but struggle to use it effectively to acquire, retain, and monetize a new generation of fans. Existing technology solutions often focus on back-office efficiencies rather than creating the engaging, personalized, and AI-native experiences that Millennial and Gen Z fans expect. This leaves a massive opportunity to reinvent the fan experience and drive top-line growth untapped.

**The Business Problem: Demonstrating Our Most Powerful AI**

Huge has developed a powerful approach to solve these challenges with our Intelligent Fan Experiences (IX) offering. However, our most impactful and relevant work is bound by NDAs with clients like Google, BBVA, and Schneider. This prevents us from showcasing our full capabilities during the sales process, slowing down our Go-To-Market efforts. Without a compelling, publicly shareable demonstration of our IX principles, we cannot effectively "show, not just tell" potential clients how we can be their strategic innovation partner. This limits our ability to accelerate the sales pipeline and capitalize on the immense opportunity in the sports, media, and entertainment sectors.

### Proposed Solution

Our solution is to create a cutting-edge, publicly-accessible AI demo that serves as a dynamic showcase for Huge's technical and creative capabilities in the AI space. This "Intelligent Fan Experience" (IX) demo, built around a fictional soccer league, will be our playground for innovation and a powerful tool for demonstrating our expertise.

**Core Concept & Approach**

The project will take the form of a **chat-based search application focused on surfacing rich media assets**. The core interaction will be a user asking a question in natural language. Our system will then:

1.  Use **vector search** to find the most relevant asset (e.g., a player's profile picture, a team logo, a short video clip) from our content library for the fictional league.
2.  Present that asset to the user in a visually compelling interface.
3.  Generate conversational "chatter" that answers the user's question and provides context or interesting commentary about the returned asset.

This "asset-first" approach will be a living lab for us to test new AI tools, develop our unique IX patterns, and build internal expertise for future client projects.

**Key Differentiators & Value**

This project is an investment in our own expertise and a platform to display our thought leadership through tangible, interactive work. Its value is twofold:

*   **Internal Growth & Learning:** It provides a low-risk environment to experiment with the latest AI advancements, particularly in vector search and multimodal generative AI. It's a space for us to learn, build, and sharpen our skills so we can move fast when a project is sold.
*   **External Showcase:** It acts as a powerful demonstration of our "tech chops" and our sophisticated understanding of AI-powered user experiences. It will be a compelling, interactive portfolio piece that proves our ability to deliver innovative solutions, moving beyond what can be shared under client NDAs.

**Vision for Success**

The ultimate vision is to create a standout demonstration of Huge's leadership in the AI-driven experience economy. This project will not only enhance our internal capabilities but will also serve as a beacon for potential clients and talent, clearly showing our ability to turn complex AI technology into elegant and impactful user experiences.

### Target Users

Our primary focus is on the **Casual Fan**. We aim to demonstrate how we can take someone with little to no knowledge of our fictional league and quickly make them feel informed, engaged, and entertained. This is a more strategic choice than targeting a "hardcore fan," as a convincing hardcore experience is impossible without the real, deep data and shared history that comes with a real league.

#### Primary User Segment: The Modern Fan (Millennial & Gen Z)

*   **Profile:**
    *   Ages 18-35, digitally native, and consumes content in short, engaging bursts across multiple platforms (TikTok, Instagram, Twitch). Their attention is fragmented, and their allegiance may be more to individual players than to teams.

*   **Current Behaviors:**
    *   They follow players and sports personalities as much as teams. They engage with sports through social media, fantasy leagues, and betting apps. They are often "second-screening" during games, looking for stats, highlights, and social commentary.

*   **Needs & Pain Points:**
    *   **Need for Context:** They can find it difficult to get up to speed on a team's history, player stats, or even the rules of the game.
    *   **Need for Personalization:** They are frustrated by generic, ad-filled sports apps and want content tailored to their specific interests (e.g., their fantasy players, specific stats, etc.).
    *   **Pain Point:** It's hard to get quick, natural-language answers to specific questions ("Who is the fastest player on this team?") without digging through multiple websites.

*   **Goals:**
    *   To feel a deeper, more interactive connection to the game.
    *   To gain social currency through their sports knowledge.
    *   To be entertained and to share that entertainment with a community.

### Goals & Success Metrics

#### Business Objectives

*   **Create a "Wow" Factor Demo:** To build a visually stunning and technically impressive demo that serves as the centerpiece of our IX sales pitch, consistently eliciting a "wow" response from potential clients.
*   **Empower the Sales Team:** To equip our sales and business development teams with a powerful, interactive tool that clearly demonstrates Huge's unique value proposition in the AI space, moving beyond static case studies.
*   **Accelerate Internal Expertise:** To use this project to test at least three new AI tools/technologies and document at least two reusable IX patterns, ensuring our delivery teams are ahead of the curve.

#### User Success Metrics (The Casual Fan)

*   **Ease of Discovery:** A user can get a meaningful, asset-based answer to a question within two interactions.
*   **Increased Engagement:** A user voluntarily asks three or more follow-up questions during a session, indicating genuine curiosity.
*   **Positive Sentiment:** In post-experience feedback, users report feeling more informed, entertained, and impressed with the experience.

#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*   **Qualitative Client Feedback:** Systematically collect feedback from prospects who have seen the demo, specifically tracking for "wow" moments and expressions of surprise or delight.
*   **Sales Team Adoption:** The demo is used in over 90% of all new IX-related sales pitches within the first quarter of its launch.
*   **On-Time Delivery:** Completion of the MVP demo within the agreed-upon project timeline.

### MVP Scope

#### Core Features (Must-Haves)

*   **Chat-based Interface:** A clean, visually appealing interface for users to type natural language questions.
*   **Vector Search on Assets:** The core engine that takes a query and finds the most relevant asset from our content library.
*   **Asset Presentation:** A dedicated area in the UI to prominently and beautifully display the returned asset.
*   **Multi-turn Conversational AI:** The ability for the AI to remember the context of the current conversation and handle follow-up questions.
*   **Simple Personalization:** A mechanism to maintain session history and basic user preferences (e.g., favorite team) to tailor the experience.
*   **"Canned" Demo Flow:** A pre-defined set of questions and interactions designed to reliably produce "wow" moments.
*   **MVP Content Set:**
    *   4 fictional teams with logos and descriptions.
    *   23 fictional players per team with profile pictures and basic attributes.

#### Out of Scope for MVP

*   **Deep or Historical Data:** No complex game-by-game stats or season-long data.
*   **Video or Audio Assets:** The MVP will focus on static images.
*   **Full User Account System:** While we will have session-based personalization, we will not have a full-fledged user account system with passwords, user profiles, etc.
*   **Organic Growth Features:** No SEO or social sharing features.

#### MVP Success Criteria

*   The application is deployed to a stable, public URL for demo purposes.
*   The core loop, including multi-turn conversations and session-based personalization, is fully functional.
*   The UI is polished and aligns with Huge's brand standards.
*   The sales team has been trained on a demo flow that showcases the personalization and conversational features.

### Post-MVP Vision

#### Phase 2 Features (The Next Steps)

*   **Richer Content:** Integrate video and audio assets (e.g., highlight clips) and add more dynamic data like game schedules and league standings.
*   **Deeper Personalization:** Evolve from session-based memory to an optional, light user account system to track a user's favorite teams and players across multiple visits.
*   **Smarter, Proactive Conversation:**
    *   Enhance the AI to be more proactive *within the app*, suggesting interesting facts or questions.
    *   Introduce **proactive push notifications** for key events (e.g., "Your favorite team just scored!").
    *   Allow for simple **comparative analysis** ("Compare player A and player B").

#### Long-Term Vision (1-2 Years)

The long-term vision is for this demo to evolve into a **fully-featured IX platform template**. It would become the foundational accelerator for all of Huge's client work in the sports and entertainment space—a "white-label" product that could be rapidly customized and deployed for real leagues, teams, or sponsors.

#### Expansion Opportunities

The core "asset-first" search and chat technology is industry-agnostic and could be adapted for other verticals like **Entertainment**, **Fashion & Retail**, or **Tourism**.

### Technical Considerations

#### Technology Stack

*   **Frontend:** The frontend stack is decided: **Vite** as the build tool, **React**, and the **shadcn/ui** component library.
*   **Backend:** The strong preference is **Python** with the **FastAPI** framework.
*   **Vector Store:** We will start with a simple, **in-memory vector store** (e.g., from LangChain) for the MVP.
*   **Large Language Model (LLM):** A powerful generative model from a provider like OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google.
*   **Hosting:** The frontend will be hosted on **Vercel**. The backend will also be hosted on Vercel if feasible.

#### Architecture Considerations

*   **Containerization:** The backend application **must be containerized using Docker**. For the frontend, we will follow Vercel's native deployment workflow, which may not involve a user-managed Docker container.
*   **Service Architecture:** A decoupled architecture is strongly recommended.
*   **Modularity First:** The architecture's primary principle will be modularity, allowing for easy swapping of components like the vector store or LLM.

### Risks & Open Questions

#### Key Risks

*   **Advanced Asset Expectation:** There may be an expectation to include advanced assets like video, which would be a significant undertaking.
*   **The "No Data" Problem:** The lack of deep, real-world historical data is a fundamental constraint that we must design around.
*   **"Wow" Factor is Subjective:** What we build, while technically sound, may not land with the desired impact during a sales pitch.
*   **LLM Performance & Consistency:** The speed and quality of the third-party LLM are outside our direct control.

#### Open Questions

*   **Defining the "Wow" Interface:** What specific UI/UX patterns and visual designs will create the "wow" factor we're aiming for? What does an "asset-first" interface look like in practice?
*   **Measuring "Wow":** What is the specific process for gathering feedback from the sales team and clients after a demo? What are the key moments in the demo we expect to be most impactful?
*   **Final Tech Stack Decisions:** Which specific LLM and other non-core technologies will we use?

#### Areas Needing Further Research

*   **UI/UX Prototyping:** Create and test 2-3 different high-fidelity prototypes for the core interface to determine which is most likely to deliver the "wow" experience.
*   **LLM Evaluation:** A formal evaluation of 2-3 different LLM providers to compare their speed, quality, and cost.
*   **Vercel for Python/FastAPI:** Confirm the best practices and any potential limitations of hosting a Python-based FastAPI backend on Vercel.
